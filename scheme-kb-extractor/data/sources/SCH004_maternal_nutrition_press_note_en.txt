PRESS NOTE (SYNTHETIC) - Department of Women and Child Development, Government of Vindhyachal
Date: 02 September 2026

Matru Poshan Sahayata Yojana launched

The State Government has launched the Matru Poshan Sahayata Yojana to support the nutrition of pregnant and lactating women.

Benefit: Eligible women will receive financial assistance as per the norms prescribed by the Department, along with a nutrition kit at the Anganwadi centre.

Who can apply: Pregnant women aged 19 years and above registered at an Anganwadi centre, for the first two live births.

Documents: Mother and Child Protection (MCP) card, Aadhaar card, bank passbook.

How to apply: Register with the Anganwadi worker of your area.

Applications must be made within 270 days of delivery. Detailed guidelines will be issued separately.
